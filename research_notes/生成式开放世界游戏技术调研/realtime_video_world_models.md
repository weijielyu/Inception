# Real-time / streaming / interactive video generation models and "interactive world models" — state as of 2026-10-09

Scope note: "real-time" here means frames are produced continuously at >=10–30 fps in response to live inputs (keyboard/controller/text/camera). Each item is dated. "Usable now" distinguishes (a) open weights, (b) hosted API/product, (c) demo only, (d) paper only. All numbers are vendor/author-reported unless stated; independent benchmarks (WorldRoamBench, WBench) are called out separately. Items marked [SUPERSEDED] have newer successors.

---

## Key Question 1: Google DeepMind Genie 2 / Genie 3 / Project Genie (and successors)

### Takeaway
Genie 3 (Aug 2025) is the reference point for the field: 720p, 24 fps, text-promptable world events, ~1 minute of visual memory, "a few minutes" of consistency. It became publicly usable (US, then global) only through Project Genie for Google AI Ultra subscribers from 29 Jan 2026, with sessions capped at 60 s and no public API; a Street View grounding mode was added 19 May 2026. No "Genie 4" was found as of Oct 2026; Waymo built a driving variant ("Waymo World Model") on it.

### Cited Findings
- Genie 2 (4 Dec 2024): single prompt image (e.g., Imagen 3 output, photos, concept art) -> action-controllable world via keyboard/mouse (WASD, Space); "consistent worlds for up to a minute, though most examples last 10-20 seconds"; showcased samples come from an *undistilled* non-real-time model, and "a distilled version can run in real time at reduced output quality"; remembers out-of-view parts of the world. [SUPERSEDED by Genie 3] — [DeepMind Genie 2 blog](https://deepmind.google/discover/blog/genie-2-a-large-scale-foundation-world-model/)
- Genie 3 announced 5 Aug 2025 (Jack Parker-Holder, Shlomi Fruchter): text-to-world, navigable in real time at 24 fps, 720p, consistency "for a few minutes", visual memory "as far back as one minute"; "first Genie model to allow real-time interaction"; generated "autoregressively, frame by frame" from world description + user actions; consistency is an "emergent capability" (no explicit 3D representation such as NeRF/Gaussian splats); supports "promptable world events" (text mid-session changes weather, adds objects/characters) — [DeepMind Genie 3 blog](https://deepmind.google/blog/genie-3-a-new-frontier-for-world-models/)
- Genie 3 stated limitations: limited action space; promptable events not necessarily performed by the agent; multi-agent interactions unsolved; cannot simulate real-world locations with geographic accuracy; legible text only when in the prompt; "a few minutes of continuous interaction, not extended hours". Released as a "limited research preview" to a small group of academics/creators; SIMA agent tested inside Genie 3 worlds — [DeepMind Genie 3 blog](https://deepmind.google/blog/genie-3-a-new-frontier-for-world-models/)
- Project Genie (29 Jan 2026): research prototype built on Genie 3, for Google AI Ultra subscribers in the US, 18+, excluding AI Ultra for Business; inputs are text prompts plus generated or uploaded images ("world sketching", with a Nano Banana Pro preview step), first- or third-person perspective; "Generations are capped at 60 seconds"; acknowledged limitations: worlds may not look true-to-life, may not follow prompts/physics, characters can be harder to control and have higher latency; promptable world events from the Aug 2025 announcement are not in this prototype — [Google blog: Project Genie](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-deepmind/project-genie/)
- 19 May 2026 (Google I/O): Project Genie connected to Street View's "280 billion images"; user picks a Maps pin (US locations), a style (e.g., Desert Sands, Stone Age, Ocean World, B&W film), describes a character; base Project Genie "rolling out today for all adult Google AI Ultra subscribers across the globe"; sessions "still run for about 60 seconds at 720p" — [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2176797/project-genie-adds-google-street-view-integration-and-goes-live-for-global-ai-ultra-users/); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/19/googles-genie-world-model-can-now-simulate-real-streets-with-street-view/)
- Waymo used Genie 3 to build the "Waymo World Model" (Feb 2026) for simulating edge cases for robotaxis — [Wikipedia: Waymo World Model](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waymo_World_Model); [The Decoder via search summary](https://introl.com/blog/world-models-race-agi-2026)
- Independent benchmark: on WorldRoamBench (Alibaba AMAP CV Lab, June 2026; 10–60 s continuous WASD/IJKL interaction, Nature/Urban/Indoor, 1st/3rd person) Genie 3 ranked first in first-person overall score (73.81), ahead of Happy Oyster (71.06) and Lyra 2.0 (70.32); "closed-source models generally did better on physics and memory"; "none reliably satisfies all dimensions, and even the best achieves only moderate scores" — [WorldRoamBench arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.31672); [HTML tables](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.31672v1)

### Inferences
- Genie 3 remains demo/consumer-product only: no developer API, no weights, 60-s capped sessions in Project Genie despite the model's claimed multi-minute capability. For a small team it is not a buildable platform in Oct 2026.
- The 60-s product cap vs. "few minutes" research claim suggests serving cost/latency limits rather than model limits.

### Gaps
- No primary DeepMind source states Genie 3 parameter count, hardware per stream, end-to-end latency, or whether a Genie 3 API is planned. No evidence found of a "Genie 4" as of Oct 2026.
- Google AI Ultra pricing was not confirmed from a primary source in this research.

## Key Question 2: Decart — Oasis (2024), MirageLSD/Mirage, Lucy 2.x, Oasis 2.0 mod, Oasis 3 (2026)

### Takeaway
Decart is the most commercially mature real-time *video-to-video* vendor: MirageLSD (July 2025, 20 fps, 768x432, <40 ms per-frame target, ~100 ms end-to-end) evolved into Lucy 2.0 (Jan 2026, 1080p/30 fps) and Lucy 2.5 (30 fps, sub-40 ms claims), sold via a pay-per-second API ($0.02/s). Its *world model* line restarted as Oasis 3 (10 June 2026) — a text-prompted, driveable, multi-camera driving simulator with "infinite" generation, API from day one at $0.02/s, but with documented drift, weak physics and limited memory. "Oasis 2.0" (late 2025) is a Minecraft mod that restyles the real game via Lucy Restyle, not a new world model.

### Cited Findings
- Oasis v1 (31 Oct 2024, with Etched): "world's first real-time AI world model ... rendered at 20 fps"; diffusion-transformer, keyboard input, autoregressive; trained on Minecraft gameplay; H100 at ~460p/20 fps with "only a few minutes of gameplay possible at a time" (InfoQ); open 500M-parameter model + code (etched-ai/open-oasis, HF Etched/oasis-500m, MIT per model card) — [Decart X post](https://x.com/DecartAI/status/1852091173420294291); [InfoQ](https://www.infoq.com/news/2024/11/decart-etched-oasis/); [open-oasis GitHub](https://github.com/etched-ai/open-oasis); [HF model card](https://huggingface.co/Etched/oasis-500m/resolve/main/README.md?download=true); [project page](https://oasis-model.github.io/)
- MirageLSD (Live Stream Diffusion), July 2025: generates each frame individually; 20 fps at 768x432 with ~100 ms per-frame latency (Wired via ETCentric); Decart's own claim "<40ms response time"; "processes only a small window of previous frames, so consistency can still decrease during longer videos"; techniques named: diffusion forcing (per-frame noise) and "history augmentation" (training on corrupted self-generated history to learn error correction); Decart's report: autoregressive errors "accumulate leading to rapid loss of quality until the frames are incoherent" — [Decart publication](https://decart.ai/publications/mirage); [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/decart-launches-miragelsd-an-ai-model-that-transforms-live-video-feeds-in-real-time/); [ETCentric](https://www.etcentric.org/decart-ais-mirage-transforms-live-stream-video-in-real-time/); [eWeek](https://www.eweek.com/news/decart-mirage-ai-live-video-rea-time-changes/)
- Lucy 2.0 (26 Jan 2026): "state-of-the-art real-time world transformation model", 1080p at 30 fps (third-party), latency claims conflict (<40 ms vs <100 ms per frame); "Smart History Augmentation" self-corrects drift, "hours of continuous generation" (third-party); optimized for AWS Trainium3 (Decart claims up to 4x frame-rate vs traditional deployments) — [Decart X post](https://x.com/DecartAI/status/2016134190509498740); [gaga.art summary](https://gaga.art/blog/decart-ai-lucy-2/)
- Lucy 2.5: live via Decart API, 30 fps, "sub-40ms latency" (one source says at 720p, another 1080p; test conditions unclear) — [HF community blog](https://huggingface.co/blog/ResterChed/lucy-2-5-real-time-ai-video); [AlphaSignal](https://alphasignal.ai/news/decart-s-lucy-2-5-transforms-live-video-at-30-fps-without-drifting)
- Decart API pricing (realtime models billed per second of active generation, 720p): Lucy 2.5 $0.02/s (fast mode $0.04/s); Lucy Restyle 2 $0.01/s; Oasis 3 Preview $0.02/s (enterprise on request); Lucy 2.1 $0.02/s; pay-as-you-go, free credits for new accounts — [Decart pricing](https://docs.platform.decart.ai/getting-started/pricing)
- Oasis 2.0 = Minecraft mod (CurseForge) that runs the real game through Decart's real-time video-to-video "Lucy Restyle"; 30 minutes granted per user — [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/oasis2)
- Oasis 3 (10 June 2026): photorealistic driving world model; "tens of frames per second"; single text prompt sets scene; outputs one front + two side camera views; "infinite generation", interaction for hours but quality degrades; auto-regressive, ~8,000 tokens per frame so "context window fills quickly"; reviewer found controls unresponsive; drift observed (NYC street became generic Western city; returning to a location showed a different environment; cars drive through cars — Decart calls physics a "major research problem"); API from day one at $0.02/s; built on Lucy + DOS (Decart Optimization Stack) on Nvidia/Amazon/Google hardware; Decart raised $300M at ~$4B valuation (Toyota, Adobe, eBay, Nvidia) — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/10/decarts-new-world-model-can-simulate-hours-of-photorealistic-driving-with-some-caveats/)
- Earlier funding: $100M Series B led by Sequoia at $3.1B (Aug 2025) — [Calcalist](https://www.calcalistech.com/ctechnews/article/m7yk4c4e3)

### Inferences
- Decart's "zero latency" marketing refers to per-frame compute (<40 ms); independently reported end-to-end latency is ~100 ms. Treat sub-40 ms as per-frame budget, not glass-to-glass.
- Oasis 3 is the only Decart *world model* a third party can call via API today; its target is AV simulation, not games, and it has no open weights.

### Gaps
- No primary Decart doc gives Oasis 3 resolution, fps, or GPU per stream. No Mirage/Lucy per-stream hardware figure is published. "Lucy Edit" and "Mirage" are absent from the current pricing page (may be deprecated/renamed).

## Key Question 3a: Other closed/commercial labs — Dynamics Lab Mirage, Odyssey, World Labs, Runway, PixVerse, Alibaba Happy Oyster, Vidu

### Takeaway
Between Oct 2025 and Oct 2026 a wave of closed real-time world models shipped as demos or request-access APIs: Odyssey-2 (20 fps, <50 ms/frame, free demo; Odyssey-2 Pro API Jan 2026; Odyssey-3 research preview 8 Oct 2026), World Labs RTFM (single H100, persistent 3D via posed-frame KV "context juggling"), Runway GWM Worlds 2 (3 Sep 2026; 720p/24 fps/48 kHz audio; "general action space"; contact-form access), PixVerse R1/R2 (Jan/Sep 2026; persistent state; playable at world.pixverse.video), and Alibaba's Happy Oyster (Apr 2026; 480p/720p, 1–3 min; fal API). None publish weights; only Happy Oyster (fal) and Odyssey (request) have third-party APIs with any pricing visibility.

### Cited Findings
**Dynamics Lab — Mirage / Mirage 2**
- Mirage (early July 2025): "first AI-native UGC game engine powered by real-time world models"; worlds modified via text, keyboard or controller; demos "Urban Chaos" (GTA-like) and "Coastal Drift" (Forza-like); reported 16 fps — [AI Brews](https://aibrews.substack.com/p/ai-native-ugc-game-engine-reasoning); [Coach Nation summary](https://coachnationamerica.com/news/dynamics-lab-launches-mirage-an-innovative-game-engine-prototype/)
- Mirage 2 (Aug 2025): continuous generation >10 minutes, 200 ms latency, runs on a single GPU; richer actions (run, jump, attack); public online demo (contrasted with Genie 3 being closed at the time) — [GIGAZINE](https://gigazine.net/gsc_news/en/20250825-ai-generative-world-mirage-2); [Delante](https://delante.co/ai-news-mirage-2/)

**Odyssey — Odyssey-2 / 2 Pro / 2 Max / 3**
- Odyssey-2 (27 Oct 2025): causal autoregressive model; "every frame generated in less than 50ms", 20 fps, multi-minute streams; text prompts steer video live, "action-aware"; converted from a bidirectional video model via multi-stage training; free demo at experience.odyssey.ml; API "coming soon" at launch; audio conditioning future work — [Odyssey blog](https://odyssey.systems/introducing-odyssey-2)
- Odyssey-2 Pro + developer API launched 23 Jan 2026 (endpoints for simulations, interactive streams, one-to-many viewable streams), later Odyssey-2 Max — [Design Offset (JP)](https://design-offset.com/20260123-odyssey-2-pro-release/); [The Rundown](https://www.therundown.ai/tools/odyssey-2)
- Pricing: "No public self-serve price; API access by request" — [The Rundown](https://www.therundown.ai/tools/odyssey-2)
- CTO (podcast): model currently predicts "1-2 minutes of coherent video" — [The Data Exchange](https://thedataexchange.media/jeff-hawke-odyssey/)
- Odyssey-3: unveiled 15 Sep 2026, research preview 8 Oct 2026 at experience.odyssey.systems; autoregressive diffusion transformer conditioned on previous observations + control inputs, with a learned action decoder for robot arms/humanoids/a car in India/a drone/playable games (separate policy per embodiment); vendor-reported Physics-IQ Verified score 66.1 for Odyssey-3 Pro; API by request, no rate card; third-party cost estimates $0.139/video (Odyssey-3), $0.267 (Pro) — [TNW](https://thenextweb.com/news/odyssey-3-world-model-robots-humanoids-cars-drones); [AlphaSignal](https://alphasignal.ai/news/odyssey-releases-odyssey-3-a-world-model-that-teaches-robots-physics-through); [OrcaRouter](https://www.orcarouter.ai/blog/odyssey-3-research-preview-launch)

**World Labs — RTFM, Marble, Atlas**
- RTFM (16 Oct 2025, research preview, demo rtfm.worldlabs.ai): "autoregressive diffusion transformer" over frame sequences, trained end-to-end on video to predict next frame; input frames become KV-cache activations; each frame carries a 3D pose (spatial memory); "context juggling" retrieves nearby posed frames so persistence is "unbounded" rather than compute-bounded; runs at interactive framerates on a single H100; accepts one or more 2D views or short real-world videos; learns reflections/shadows/lens flare from data; dynamic worlds and user interaction with generated content listed as *future work*; no fps/resolution figures given — [World Labs RTFM blog](https://www.worldlabs.ai/blog/rtfm)
- Marble (beta Sep 2025, commercial launch Nov 2025): produces persistent, downloadable 3D environments (Gaussian splats, meshes, video) from text/image/multi-image/video; static spaces, no characters/dynamics — i.e., not a real-time frame generator — [Yahoo/TechCrunch](https://tech.yahoo.com/ai/meta-ai/articles/fei-fei-li-world-labs-134401396.html); [Radiance Fields](https://radiancefields.com/world-labs-announces-realtime-frame-model-(rtfm))
- Atlas announced 1 Sep 2026 as an "omni" spatial-intelligence model trained on text/images/video/3D; no evidence of real-time playability found — [Awesome-Interactive-World-Model list](https://github.com/YunqiaoYang/Awesome-Interactive-World-Model)

**Runway — Game Worlds, GWM-1, GWM Worlds 2**
- Game Worlds (2025): initially text + static images, a text-adventure style product — [AIbase](https://www.aibase.com/news/19325)
- GWM-1 (11 Dec 2025): Runway's first "General World Model", pixel-by-pixel frame prediction, positioned for robotics/life-science agent training — [Yahoo/TechCrunch](https://tech.yahoo.com/ai/meta-ai/articles/runway-releases-first-world-model-170000394.html)
- GWM Worlds 2 (3 Sep 2026, research preview): "interactive real-time simulations in continuous 720p video at 24 fps with 48000 Hz audio"; autoregressive diffusion model built on Runway's audio-visual generation model; users steer via text actions aimed at any subject or the scene plus continuous camera motion; "no preset length"; multiplayer roles (Player 1 / Director); claimed "first world model with a general action space, merging navigation, manipulation and human interaction"; limitations: only the first frame can be an image reference, no guaranteed consistent world state, textures/geometry drift with fast camera moves; access via contact form, no pricing/API — [Runway Research](https://runway.com/research/introducing-gwm-worlds-2); [Runway X](https://x.com/runwayml/status/2095540014645920040); [Germanidis X](https://x.com/agermanidis/status/2095597719574466676); [AlphaSignal](https://alphasignal.ai/news/runway-s-gwm-worlds-2-streams-interactive-ai-worlds-at-720p-in-real-time)

**PixVerse — R1 / R2**
- R1 launched Jan 2026 (first PixVerse real-time world model); R2 announced 22 Sep 2026 (beta since July 2026): "Omni Causal AR" backbone + real-time acceleration layer; inputs text, reference images, audio, action controls (WASD move, arrow keys camera); mid-play prompts alter environment; state persists across session; PixVerse Game Engine (July 2026) runs on R2; worlds viewable at world.pixverse.video; fps/resolution/latency/pricing not stated — [PixVerse blog](https://pixverse.ai/en/blog/pixverse-introduces-r2-real-time-world-model)

**Alibaba — Happy Oyster (Taotian Future Life Lab / Token Hub)**
- Unveiled April 2026; modes: Directing (steer a live scene with text/voice/images; 480p or 720p; up to 3 min) and Wandering (first-person WASD + camera; 480p; up to 1 min); joint audio-video output; available via fal playground and API (text-to-world, image-to-world, explore endpoints; pay-per-second, rates on model page); not open-weight — [fal.ai Happy Oyster](https://fal.ai/happy-oyster); [The Neuron](https://www.theneurondaily.com/p/two-free-3d-world-models-dropped-this-week)
- WorldRoamBench: Happy Oyster second in first-person overall (71.06) and strongest control rate/subject memory in third person — [WorldRoamBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.31672)

**Shengshu — Vidu S1 / S2**
- Vidu S1 (July 2026 paper): "A Real-Time Interactive Video Generation Model" — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.03118)
- Vidu S2 (10 Sep 2026): S2-Avatar (real-time interactive digital character, 720p, dynamic references updatable at any moment) and S2-Editing (real-time style/clothing/character/background replacement); online demo vidu.com/vidu-stream — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11638); [Vidu Stream](https://www.vidu.com/vidu-stream)

### Inferences
- Across closed labs, the common recipe is: large bidirectional video model -> converted to causal/autoregressive -> few-step distilled -> served on one H100-class GPU per stream at 20–24 fps, 480p–720p.
- Only Happy Oyster (fal) and Decart publish per-second prices; Odyssey/Runway/World Labs/PixVerse are request-access or consumer demos.

### Gaps
- No fps/resolution/latency numbers for RTFM, PixVerse R2, Happy Oyster, or Mirage 2 from primary sources. Odyssey-2 resolution is not stated. Runway GWM Worlds 2 hardware per stream is unknown.
- Happy Oyster per-second fal price not captured (on model page, not fetched).

## Key Question 3b: Open-weights / open-research real-time systems (Tencent, Skywork, Krea, NVIDIA LongLive, Self-Forcing, Rolling Forcing, StreamDiffusionV2, Yume, Lingbot, Zing, ABot, minWM, Infinite-World, etc.)

### Takeaway
By Oct 2026 a small team can run real-time interactive world models locally: Tencent HY-World 1.5/WorldPlay (24 fps, 480p, 8B/5B, Hunyuan community license), Skywork Matrix-Game 2.0/3.0 (MIT; 3.0 claims up to 40 fps at 720p with a 5B model), Krea Realtime 14B (Apache-2.0; 11 fps on B200), NVIDIA LongLive (Apache-2.0; 20.7 fps 1.3B, LongLive 2.0 5B up to 45.7 fps), Self-Forcing (16–17 fps on H100, ~10 fps on 4090), Zing-0.5 (5B, 480p, 24 fps, weights released), Yume-5B (12 fps at 540p on A100), Lingbot-World (16 fps, <1 s latency), and StreamDiffusionV2 (training-free streaming pipeline, 58 fps with 14B on 4xH100). Hunyuan-GameCraft-2 (16 fps, 14B MoE) and Matrix-Game 3.5 have papers but no confirmed weights.

### Cited Findings
**Tencent Hunyuan**
- Hunyuan-GameCraft 1.0 (paper June 2025; code + weights released 14 Aug 2025; HF tencent/Hunyuan-GameCraft-1.0, checkpoint folder 60.3 GB); keyboard/mouse unified into a continuous camera action space; hybrid history conditioning — [GitHub](https://github.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/Hunyuan-GameCraft-1.0); [arXiv 2506.17201](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.17201)
- Hunyuan-GameCraft-2 (arXiv 28 Nov 2025): instruction-following (text + keyboard + mouse; e.g., "open the door", "trigger an explosion"); 14B image-to-video MoE diffusion backbone; "16 FPS" after acceleration; KV-recache; randomized long-video tuning; limitations: "semantic drift still appears in sequences longer than 500 frames", no explicit long-term memory, mostly single-step immediate-effect actions, latency still too high for "highly reactive gameplay"; introduces InterBench. No code/weights link found on project page or Tencent-Hunyuan GitHub org as of Oct 2026 — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.23429); [project page](https://hunyuan-gamecraft-2.github.io/)
- HY-World 1.5 / WorldPlay (17 Dec 2025): streaming video diffusion, 24 fps, text or single image + keyboard/mouse (w/a/s/d, arrows) or JSON camera trajectories; next-chunk prediction in 16-frame chunks; "Reconstituted Context Memory" + temporal reframing for long-term geometric consistency; "Context Forcing" distillation (4-step); WorldPlay-8B (HunyuanVideo-based) and WorldPlay-5B (Wan-based); checkpoints are 480P-I2V; VRAM: AR distilled inference 28 GB (sp=8) / 34 GB (sp=4) / 72 GB (sp=1), training 60 GB; default 125 frames, no max stated; full training stack (pre-train, mid-train, RL post-train "WorldCompass", distillation) released; 1.6k stars; license: Tencent Hunyuan Community License (third-party) — [HY-WorldPlay GitHub](https://github.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/HY-WorldPlay); [X summary](https://x.com/kimmonismus/status/2001250488130658356)
- HY-World 2.0 (16 Apr 2026): multimodal world model producing persistent 3D assets (meshes, Gaussian splats, point clouds) for Unity/Unreal/Isaac Sim; staged weight drops (WorldMirror 2.0 Apr 16, HY-Pano 2.0 May 11, world-generation code + WorldStereo May 18); custom tencent-hy-world-2.0-community license; WorldNav weights reportedly missing — [GitHub](https://github.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/HY-World-2.0); [HF](https://huggingface.co/tencent/HY-World-2.0)
- WorldPlay2 (arXiv 28 Sep 2026): "Extending Real-Time Interactive World Models in Control and Horizon"; compact memory tokens shared by AR student and bidirectional teacher; "Stable Forcing" with full-rollout replay; factorized control (frame-aligned actions + semantic control over scene/character/events); no fps/code stated — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35560)

**Skywork — Matrix-Game 2.0 / 3.0 / 3.5**
- Matrix-Game 2.0 (12 Aug 2025): "first open-source, real-time, long-sequence interactive world model"; 25 fps, minute-long videos; few-step autoregressive diffusion with causal attention; frame-level mouse/keyboard; trained on ~1,200 h (paper) / 1,350 h (X) of Unreal Engine + GTA5 data; parameter count reported as 1.8B (HF summary) vs "1.3B" (Skywork X) — conflicting — [arXiv 2508.13009](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.13009); [Skywork X](https://x.com/Skywork_ai/status/1955237399912648842); [aimodels HF summary](https://www.aimodels.fyi/models/huggingFace/matrix-game-2.0-skywork)
- Repo license MIT; releases: 1.0 (12 May 2025), 2.0 (12 Aug 2025), 3.0 (27 Mar 2026); 2.3k stars — [SkyworkAI/Matrix-Game GitHub](https://github.com/SkyworkAI/Matrix-Game)
- Matrix-Game 3.0 (arXiv 10 Apr 2026, v3 29 Sep 2026): memory-augmented, "up to 40 FPS real-time generation at 720p resolution with a 5B model"; a 2x14B scaled variant; camera-aware memory retrieval/injection; "stable memory consistency over minute-long sequences" — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.08995)
- Matrix-Game 3.5 (arXiv 30 Aug 2026): "patch memory" = explicit 3D patch retrieval + projective camera conditioning (tiled PRoPE), no added parameters; static-dynamic disentangled representation; two-stage progressive distillation to few-step causal generator; "minute-long real-time interactive generation"; code/weights not confirmed — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29910)

**Krea Realtime 14B** (20 Oct 2025)
- Distilled from Wan 2.1 14B T2V via Self-Forcing; ~11 fps text-to-video with 4 steps on one B200; ~1 s to first frame; mid-generation prompt changes, video-to-video from uploads/webcam/canvas, LoRA; mitigations "KV Cache Recomputation" and "KV Cache Attention Bias" for error accumulation; HF model card license Apache-2.0 (GitHub README says CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 — conflicting; verify before commercial use); recommended >=40 GB VRAM (third party) — [HF model card](https://huggingface.co/krea/krea-realtime-video); [GitHub](https://github.com/krea-ai/realtime-video); [Krea X](https://x.com/krea_ai/status/1980358158376988747)

**Self-Forcing (Xun Huang et al., NeurIPS 2025 Spotlight)**
- Trains AR video diffusion by simulating inference (AR rollout with KV cache) during training; ~17 fps sub-second latency on one H100 (paper), ~16 fps H100 / ~10 fps RTX 4090 at 480p with ~0.8 s initial latency (project page); frame-wise variant latency 0.45 s; built on Wan2.1-1.3B; Deep Forcing re-measured 15.78 fps / 0.77 s on H100 for 60-s videos — [project page](https://self-forcing.github.io/); [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.08009v1); [GitHub](https://github.com/guandeh17/Self-Forcing); [Deep Forcing](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2512.05081)
- CausVid (DMD + diffusion forcing, the predecessor): measured on A800 at 10.4 fps / 0.60 s latency (same as Self-Forcing on that hardware) — [Causal Forcing++](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.15141)

**NVIDIA LongLive** (29 Sep 2025; ICLR 2026)
- 1.3B, 20.7 fps (LongLive 1.0); LongLive 2.0 is 5B at 24.8–45.7 fps (GPU not named in README); KV-recache on prompt switch, streaming long tuning, attention sink; interactive sequential prompts; Apache-2.0; paper table lists 832x480 — [GitHub](https://github.com/NVlabs/LongLive); [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.22622v1)

**Rolling Forcing** (Sep 2025; ICLR 2026)
- Joint denoising of a rolling window at increasing noise levels, attention sink on initial frames, windowed distillation; "real-time streaming generation of multi-minute videos on a single GPU", 16 fps; critique: sink+rolling cache is a crude memory — middle history is forgotten — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.25161v1); [project page](https://kunhao-liu.github.io/Rolling_Forcing_Webpage/); [Pith review](https://pith.science/paper/2509.25161)

**StreamDiffusionV2** (Nov 2025; MLSys 2026 Best Paper)
- Training-free streaming system: SLO-aware batching, block scheduler, sink-token-guided rolling KV cache, motion-aware noise controller; pipeline parallel across steps/layers; first frame <0.5 s; 58.28 fps with a 14B model and 64.52 fps with 1.3B on 4xH100 (no TensorRT/quant); 1–4 denoising steps; open source — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.07399); [GitHub](https://github.com/chenfengxu714/StreamDiffusionV2); [project page](https://streamdiffusionv2.github.io/)

**Yume / Yume 1.5** (Shanghai AI Lab + Fudan; CVPR 2026)
- Text- or image-to-world with keyboard control and text event editing; Yume-5B released 26 Dec 2025 (HF stdstu123/Yume-5B-720P); 12 fps at 540p on a single A100; ranked 5th first-person Visual Score but 10th Action and last Memory on WorldRoamBench — [arXiv 2512.22096](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.22096v1); [GitHub](https://github.com/stdstu12/YUME); [HF](https://huggingface.co/stdstu123/Yume-5B-720P); [WorldRoamBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.31672)

**Lingbot-World** (Robbyant Team, 28 Jan 2026): open-sourced world simulator; 16 fps, latency <1 s, "minute-level horizon"; code + model public, license unspecified — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.20540)

**Zing-0.5** (seedleap, 15 Sep 2026): 5B AR world model built on Wan2.2-TI2V-5B; 832x480 at 24 fps with 4-step generation; magnitude-aware WASD/IJKL + time-aligned text instructions; ~USD 0.009 per stream-minute server cost; WBench Navigation 81.0 overall / 88.5 consistency; weights, inference code, and Zing-SGLang serving released; authors note state/rule persistence remain open — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17909)

**ABot-World-0** (21 Jul 2026): 720p streamed at up to 16 fps on one RTX 5090 (~19 GiB peak VRAM); 1.2 s action-to-first-frame; bidirectional teacher distilled into causal student (teacher forcing + ODE distillation + "LongForcing"); raw keyboard actions, reference-character memory for third person; reported open weights on HF + browser demo (secondary) — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19191); [search summary](https://worldsimulator.ai/blog/articles/best-ai-world-models)

**minWM** (shengshu-ai, 28 May 2026; v2 22 Sep 2026): full-stack open pipeline converting bidirectional T2V/TI2V models (Wan2.1-1.3B, HY1.5-TI2V-8B) into camera-controllable few-step AR world models via Causal Forcing / Causal Forcing++ with asymmetric DMD; scripts, checkpoints, streaming inference released — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.30263)

**Infinite-World** (Feb 2026; ICML 2026): Hierarchical Pose-free Memory Compressor distills history latents into fixed budget; "1000+ frame" horizons; no fps/code stated — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.02393)

**Other 2026 open/academic entries (from the community survey list; mostly paper + code, verify weights individually)**: HelixWorld (29 Sep 2026; real-time audio-visual world model, 24 fps, 6-DoF camera + actions, spatial stereo audio, single GPU) — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38123); AlayaWorld (Jul 2026; long-horizon playable, full-stack open framework) — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.06291); MIRA multiplayer world models (Jul 2026) — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.05352); WanToFight real-time multi-player combat (Jul 2026) — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.12592); Solaris multiplayer Minecraft (Feb 2026, 2602.22208), LIVE (ICML'26, 2602.03747), SANA-WM minute-scale (2605.15178), Gamma-World multi-agent (2605.28816), GameDirector (Sep 2026) — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.25652); DreamForge-World 0.1 low-compute real-time — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.30292); full list at [Awesome-Interactive-World-Model](https://github.com/YunqiaoYang/Awesome-Interactive-World-Model)

**LTX / Wan real-time variants**
- LTX-2 (19B, Jan 2026): 1.22 s per diffusion step on H100 for 121 frames at 720p vs 22.30 s for Wan 2.2-14B; up to 20 s with stereo audio; open weights on HF; a clip generator, not an interactive streamer — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2601.03233); [HF](https://huggingface.co/Lightricks/LTX-2)

### Inferences
- Nearly every open real-time model is a few-step causal distillation of Wan 2.1/2.2 (1.3B/5B/14B) or HunyuanVideo (8B); Self-Forcing/Causal Forcing + DMD is the de facto recipe; 480p at 16–25 fps on one H100/4090-class GPU is the typical open-weights operating point, with 720p at 40 fps (Matrix-Game 3.0, 5B) the top open claim.

## Key Question 6: Key enabling techniques and their failure modes; biggest unsolved problems

### Takeaway
Real-time generation rests on: (1) converting bidirectional video DiTs to causal/block-causal attention with KV-cache rollout; (2) few-step distillation (DMD -> CausVid -> Self-Forcing -> Causal Forcing/++ , Context Forcing, LongForcing, Stable Forcing) trained on self-generated rollouts to close the train–test gap; (3) diffusion forcing / per-frame noise and history augmentation to tolerate corrupted context; (4) rolling KV windows with attention sinks, KV-recache on prompt switches; (5) explicit long-term memory (posed-frame retrieval, 3D patch memory, reconstituted context, hierarchical compression, memory tokens); (6) systems work (SLO-aware batching, pipeline parallelism, FP8/SageAttention, ASICs/Trainium). Failure modes: error accumulation/drift, forgetting of out-of-view content, weak physics (collisions, clipping), poor mid-stream event controllability, limited action spaces, multi-agent incoherence.

### Cited Findings
- Causal conversion + KV cache: RTFM stores frames as KV activations and retrieves posed neighbors ("context juggling") for unbounded persistence — [World Labs](https://www.worldlabs.ai/blog/rtfm); Odyssey-2 multi-stage conversion from bidirectional to causal — [Odyssey](https://odyssey.systems/introducing-odyssey-2); Matrix-Game 2.0 argues bidirectional attention and many steps preclude real time — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.13009)
- Distillation lineage: CausVid = diffusion forcing + DMD but "training outputs ... do not come from the distribution the model produces at inference time"; Self-Forcing fixes this by AR rollout with KV cache during training — [Self-Forcing arXiv](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.08009v1); Causal Forcing (Feb 2026) and Causal Forcing++ (May 2026) refine distillation for interactive generation (asymmetric DMD, used by minWM) — [arXiv 2602.02214](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.02214); [arXiv 2605.15141](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.15141); [minWM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.30263); Context Forcing aligns memory context between teacher and student (HY-WorldPlay) — [GitHub](https://github.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/HY-WorldPlay); LongForcing aligns long student rollouts with an extended-horizon teacher (ABot-World-0) — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19191); Stable Forcing with full-rollout replay (WorldPlay2) — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35560); two-stage progressive distillation (Matrix-Game 3.5) — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29910)

### Inferences
- The field's consensus trajectory (Matrix-Game 3.5, RTFM, HY-World 2.0, Lyra 2.0, "From Pixels to States", GameDirector) is toward hybrid designs: a causal video generator for rendering plus an explicit spatial/state memory (posed frames, 3D patches, Gaussian scaffolds, or symbolic game state) to deliver object permanence and rule persistence that pure KV-cache models lack.
- Mid-stream *event* control (text instructions that change the world) is now standard in product demos (Genie 3, GameCraft-2, Yume, Zing, Runway, PixVerse) but remains limited to single-step immediate effects; persistent rules/inventory/NPC logic are unsolved.

### Gaps
- No paper found that quantifies object permanence over multi-minute horizons on a shared benchmark across closed models (Genie 3 appears only in WorldRoamBench).
- Multi-agent/multiplayer coherence (Solaris, MIRA, WanToFight, Gamma-World, Runway multiplayer roles) has no independent evaluation yet.
