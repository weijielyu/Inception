Scope note: findings below are from web searches/fetches of arXiv pages, GitHub READMEs, official docs, and (where flagged) third-party aggregators. Each item is dated. Status tags used throughout: **[OPEN]** = open weights usable now; **[API]** = hosted API/product usable now; **[RESEARCH]** = paper/demo only, no public weights found. Where I could not verify a number from a primary source it is marked *(secondary)*.

## KQ1. Egocentric video generation: models, datasets, resolution/length, hands/body

### Takeaway
By mid-2026 there is a clear research line of "egocentric world simulators" that take **one scene image plus a user's body/hand/head motion** and autoregressively generate first-person video with hands in frame (PlayerOne, EgoControl, Hand2World, Generated Reality, HandsOnWorld, EgoSim, EgoGenesis). However, almost all of them are **[RESEARCH]** — I found no released weights for any of them except that EgoVid-5M (dataset) and Yume (keyboard-only, no hands) are public. Nothing egocentric-with-hands is usable off-the-shelf today; the practical path is a game/world model (Matrix-Game, HY-WorldPlay, GameCraft) that is first-person but hand-less.

### Cited Findings

**Datasets**
- EgoVid-5M (Alibaba/CASIA/Tsinghua; arXiv Nov 2024; NeurIPS 2025 D&B track): >5M egocentric clips at **1080p**, each with high-level text action descriptions plus fine-grained kinematic control signals; split given as 5M textual descriptions and 65K kinematic annotations. Built by cleaning Ego4D-style footage for frame consistency / action coherence / motion smoothness. Also proposes **EgoDreamer**, generating egocentric video from action text + kinematic signals; baselines include U-Net and DiT models. — [arXiv 2411.08380](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.08380); [NeurIPS 2025](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/3ef2b740cb22dcce67c20989cb3d3fce-Abstract-Datasets_and_Benchmarks_Track.html). My fetch of the Hugging Face dataset page returned **HTTP 401** (gated/auth-required), so download terms are unverified — [HF dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Alibaba-Research-Intelligence-Computing/EgoVid-5M).
- EgoVid-Pro (Jul 2026, HandsOnWorld paper): 103K clips, ~12M frames (~117 frames/clip) of protagonist-only hand trajectories distilled from in-the-wild monocular video via 3-level filtering (action semantics, image quality, 3D geometry). — [arXiv 2607.02075](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02075)
- Ego4D: paper reports 3,025 hours from 855 camera wearers, 74 locations, 9 countries; repo now cites 3,700+ hours. — [facebookresearch/Ego4d](https://github.com/facebookresearch/Ego4d). License: one vendor blog says Ego4D permits certain commercial ML uses but not redistribution/resale of footage; another describes it as research-only, application required — conflict, read the official license. — [cloudpano blog](https://www.cloudpano.com/blog/is-ego4d-free-for-commercial-use); contradicted by [claru.ai](https://claru.ai/compare/ego4d-alternative)
- Ego-Exo4D: V2 release 1,286.30 video-hours (221.26 ego-hours) across 5,035 takes; time-synced Aria-glasses ego + GoPro exo views; 800+ participants (abstract versions give 1,286 vs 1,422 h). — [arXiv 2311.18259](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.18259v4); [Meta blog](https://ai.meta.com/blog/ego-exo4d-video-learning-perception/)
- EPIC-KITCHENS: original 55 h / 11.5M frames / 39.6K action segments; distributed under CC BY-NC 4.0 *(secondary)*. — [arXiv 2005.00343](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.00343v1); [cloudpano](https://www.cloudpano.com/blog/egocentric-video-datasets-commercial-model-training)

**Models (chronological)**
- Navigation World Models (Bar, Zhou, Tran, Darrell, LeCun; CVPR 2025) **[RESEARCH/code]**: Conditional Diffusion Transformer (CDiT) up to 1B params, trained on egocentric video of humans and robots; predicts future observations from past frames + navigation actions; used for trajectory planning by simulation. — [CVPR 2025](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025/html/Bar_Navigation_World_Models_CVPR_2025_paper.html). Follow-ups: RAE-NWM (Tsinghua, Mar 2026) models dynamics in dense DINOv2 feature space with time-driven action gating — [arXiv 2603.09241](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.09241); MWM (PKU, Mar 2026) adds Action-Conditioned Consistency post-training to stop multi-step drift — [arXiv 2603.07799](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.07799).
- **PlayerOne: Egocentric World Simulator** (HKU + Alibaba DAMO; arXiv Jun 11 2025, v3 Dec 10 2025) **[RESEARCH]**: input = one egocentric scene image + the user's real body motion captured by an **exocentric camera**; "part-disentangled motion injection" for part-level control; coarse-to-fine training (pretrain on large egocentric text-video pairs, finetune on synchronous ego-exo motion-video pairs built by an automatic pipeline); joint reconstruction framework progressively models the 4D scene + frames for long-form consistency. Resolution/length not stated on the abstract page; no code/weights link found. — [arXiv 2506.09995](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.09995); project page playerone-hku.github.io
- **EgoControl** (Univ. Bonn / Lamarr / NVIDIA; arXiv Nov 2025; CVPR 2026) **[RESEARCH]**: pose-controllable egocentric video diffusion; conditions future frames on explicit **3D full-body pose sequences** (captures global camera + articulated body); input = short observed frame sequence + target poses. — [arXiv 2511.18173](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.18173); [NVIDIA DAIR page](https://research.nvidia.com/labs/dair/publication/egocontrol/)
- **Hand2World** (NTU; arXiv Feb 2026) **[RESEARCH]**: egocentric interaction generation from a **single scene image** driven by free-space hand gestures; occlusion-invariant hand conditioning from projected 3D hand meshes; per-pixel Plücker-ray camera embedding; bidirectional diffusion distilled to a causal generator for arbitrary-length synthesis. — [arXiv 2602.09600](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.09600v1)
- **Generated Reality** (Xie, Sun, Neall, Wu, Cai, Wetzstein; arXiv Feb 20 2026, v2 Aug 2026) **[RESEARCH]**: interactive egocentric simulator conditioned on tracked **head pose + joint-level hand poses**; bidirectional teacher distilled into causal few-step AR model; supports dexterous hand-object interaction; user studies show higher task performance and sense of control vs baselines. Resolution/fps/latency not on abstract page. — [arXiv 2602.18422](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.18422); project page codeysun.github.io/generated-reality
- **EgoSim** (SJTU / Shanghai AI Lab; arXiv Apr 2026) **[RESEARCH]**: closed-loop egocentric world simulator that generates spatially consistent interaction videos and **persistently updates an explicit 3D scene state**, to avoid structural drift and the "static scene" assumption. — [arXiv 2604.01001](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.01001)
- **Wh0** (arXiv Jun 2026) **[RESEARCH]**: uses generative world models as scalable sources of egocentric hand-manipulation data for robot learning. — [arXiv 2606.22136](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.22136)
- **HandsOnWorld** (Chen, Shi, Wu, Wang, Wan, Liu; arXiv Jul 2 2026, v3 Aug 14 2026) **[RESEARCH]**: "Plücker Hand Map" extends Plücker rays from camera to the hand surface so hand motion and ego-motion are disentangled in one world frame; trained on EgoVid-Pro; claims better fidelity/control accuracy and in-the-wild generalization. No code link on abstract page. — [arXiv 2607.02075](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02075)
- **EgoGenesis** (arXiv Jul 2026) **[RESEARCH]**: "Egocentric World-Action Modeling with Online Anchored Projective Memory and Action-3D RoPE" — title only; not fetched. — [arXiv 2607.28243](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.28243)
- Survey: "Hand-Object Interaction in the Age of Large Foundation Models: Reconstruction, Generation, and Embodied Transfer" (Jul 2026) covers this space. — [arXiv 2607.28394](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.28394)
- Yume (Shanghai AI Lab; see KQ2) is egocentric exploration from one image but **does not model hands/body**; it is the only egocentric-style model in this group with released weights. — [GitHub stdstu12/YUME](https://github.com/stdstu12/YUME)

### Inferences
- The field has converged on a recipe: Wan/HunyuanVideo-class DiT → add camera (Plücker) + hand/body pose conditioning → distill bidirectional teacher into a causal few-step AR student for interactivity (Hand2World, Generated Reality, GameCraft, Matrix-Game, HY-WorldPlay all do this). A team wanting hands-in-frame egocentric generation would need to replicate this on EgoVid-5M/Ego-Exo4D since no weights are public.
- For the game use case ("I walk forward / start digging / pick something up"), the hand-conditioned models require **tracked hand/body poses as input** (from a VR headset or exo camera), not keyboard actions; mapping discrete game actions to pose trajectories would be an additional engineering layer.
- Dataset licensing (Ego4D/Ego-Exo4D need license agreements; EPIC-KITCHENS is NC) is a practical blocker for commercial fine-tuning; EgoVid-5M is gated.

### Gaps
- Output resolution, clip length, fps and GPU requirements for PlayerOne, EgoControl, Hand2World, Generated Reality, HandsOnWorld, EgoSim were not on the abstract pages I fetched; full PDFs not read.
- "EgoGen" (the CVPR 2024 synthetic egocentric generator) returned no search hits in my queries; not covered.
- Whether EgoVid-5M actually ships video files or only metadata/pointers into Ego4D could not be verified (HF page returned 401).

## KQ2. Camera/action-controllable generation (keyboard/mouse/camera trajectory, latent actions); which accept an arbitrary user photo as start

### Takeaway
Several **open-weight image-to-world models** now accept an arbitrary first-frame image and keyboard/mouse actions in (near) real time: Matrix-Game 2.0 (MIT, ≥24 GB VRAM, 25 fps), Matrix-Game 3.0 (720p/40 fps but needs 9 GPUs), HY-WorldPlay / HY-World 1.5 (480p/24 fps, 28–72 GB), Yume-5B (720p, Apache-2.0, runs on 16 GB laptop GPU slowly), Hunyuan-GameCraft-1.0 (24 GB min / 80 GB recommended). Camera-trajectory-only models (GEN3C, Voyager, CameraCtrl family) are open but offline (minutes per clip). Genie 3, Odyssey-2 and GameCraft-2 are the strongest but are research-preview/API-only or unreleased.

### Cited Findings

**Keyboard/mouse "game world models"**
- **Hunyuan-GameCraft 1.0** (Tencent Hunyuan + HUST; paper arXiv Jun 2025; weights Aug 14 2025) **[OPEN]**: built on HunyuanVideo; WASD/arrow keys mapped into a unified continuous camera space (3 translation + 2 rotation axes, no roll; key-press duration = speed); hybrid history conditioning extends video autoregressively in ~1.3 s chunks; trained on >1M gameplay recordings from 100+ AAA games then finetuned on synthetic annotated data; 55% interaction-error reduction and **6.6 FPS** real-time *(secondary, alphaXiv summary)*. Official requirements: NVIDIA GPU, **minimum 24 GB VRAM (very slow), 80 GB recommended**, Linux. — [HF paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2506.17201); [the-decoder](https://the-decoder.com/tencents-hunyuan-gamecraft-transforms-single-images-into-interactive-gaming-videos/); [HF weights tencent/Hunyuan-GameCraft-1.0](https://huggingface.co/tencent/Hunyuan-GameCraft-1.0)
- **Hunyuan-GameCraft-2** (arXiv Nov 28 2025, v2 Feb 10 2026) **[RESEARCH / hosted demo]**: 14B image-to-video MoE foundation; controlled by **natural-language prompts + keyboard + mouse**; text-driven interaction injection for camera motion, character behavior and environment dynamics; automatic pipeline converting large text–video corpora into interactive datasets; introduces **InterBench**. Secondary sources claim 16 FPS via FP8 + parallel VAE decode and trigger rate 0.983 for actor actions. No GitHub/weights found; project page links a demo at hunyuan.tencent.com/game/game-craft (unclear whether it serves v2). — [arXiv 2511.23429](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.23429); [project page](https://hunyuan-gamecraft-2.github.io/); [gaga.art summary](https://gaga.art/blog/hunyuan-gamecraft-2/)
- **Matrix-Game 2.0** (Skywork AI; Aug 12 2025) **[OPEN, MIT]**: few-step AR diffusion, **25 fps**, minute-long sequences, frame-level keyboard+mouse; data pipeline on Unreal Engine + game environments; third-party says ~1.8B params, 640×352 *(secondary)*. README: ≥24 GB VRAM (A100/H100 tested), 64 GB RAM, Linux; weights for universal scenes and TempleRun; `inference.py --img_path` takes an input image (image-to-world). — [arXiv 2508.13009](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.13009); [GitHub README](https://github.com/SkyworkAI/Matrix-Game/blob/main/Matrix-Game-2/README.md); [GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2025/08/11/3131160/0/en/Matrix-Game-2-0-Released-The-First-Open-Source-Interactive-World-Model-for-Real-Time-Long-Sequence-Generation.html); [toolnavs](https://toolnavs.com/en/article/70-matrix-game-20-18b-parameter-open-source-world-model-for-real-time-game-generati)
- **Matrix-Game 3.0** (Skywork; released Mar 27 2026; arXiv 2604.08995) **[OPEN, MIT repo]**: 5B real-time model at **720p up to 40 FPS** using **8 GPUs for DiT + 1 GPU for VAE**; a 2×14B scaled variant; few-step sampling, INT8 DiT attention, MG-LightVAE (up to 5.2× faster decode); training on Video-Pose-Action-Prompt data from Unreal Engine, AAA games, and real video; memory described in KQ3. — [project page](https://matrix-game-v3.github.io/); [GitHub](https://github.com/SkyworkAI/Matrix-Game)
- **HY-WorldPlay / HY-World 1.5** (Tencent; Dec 17 2025; WAN-5B variant + 8B training code Jan 6 2026; RL post-training "WorldCompass" Mar 8 2026) **[OPEN]**: streaming video diffusion in 16-frame chunks; I2V from a single image or T2V; **480p, 24 FPS**; "Dual Action Representation" (keyboard pose strings `w/a/s/d/up/down/left/right` + duration, or JSON camera trajectories); 8B HunyuanVideo-1.5 backbone (lightweight 5B WAN pipeline with reduced control/memory); VRAM for 125-frame distilled AR generation: **~28 GB (sp=8), 34 GB (sp=4), 72 GB (sp=1)**; variants: bidirectional, AR, AR+RL, 4-step distilled. License file present but terms not extracted. — [GitHub Tencent-Hunyuan/HY-WorldPlay](https://github.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/HY-WorldPlay)
- **Yume** (Shanghai AI Lab; Yume-1.0 Jul 23 2025; Yume-5B-720P + Yume-I2V-540P weights and Yume-1.5 paper Dec 26 2025) **[OPEN, Apache-2.0]**: image or text → explorable world; camera trajectories quantized into discrete keyboard controls (W etc.) + mouse; text captions per 2-second segment control events; Yume-1.5 reports **12 fps at 540p on one A100** (CVPR 2026); Windows demo tested on RTX 4090 Laptop 16 GB (≥16 GB recommended); training needs ≥16 A100s; codebase reuses Wan2.1; I2V takes a directory of user JPGs. Yume-1.5 weights themselves not confirmed released as of the README. — [GitHub stdstu12/YUME](https://github.com/stdstu12/YUME); [arXiv 2512.22096](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.22096v1); [CVPR 2026 poster](https://cvpr.thecvf.com/virtual/2026/poster/36968)
- **Genie** (DeepMind, Feb 2024): 11B foundation world model trained from unlabeled Internet video via a **latent action model** (VQ codebook of inferred inter-frame actions), video tokenizer, and dynamics model; LAM discarded at inference and replaced by user actions. — [arXiv 2402.15391](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.15391)
- **Genie 3** (DeepMind, Aug 5 2025) **[RESEARCH preview]**: **720p, 24 fps**, real-time navigation, worlds consistent for "a few minutes", visual memory ~1 minute, promptable world events; limitations: limited action space, multi-agent interaction unsolved, no exact geographic accuracy, legible text only when prompted; access = limited research preview for a small cohort; page links "Try Project Genie" (terms not described). — [DeepMind blog](https://deepmind.google/discover/blog/genie-3-a-new-frontier-for-world-models/)
- **AdaWorld** (Mar 2025): latent-action world model initialized from Stable Video Diffusion. — [arXiv 2503.18938](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2503.18938)
- **Vid2World** (Tsinghua; arXiv May 2025; ICLR 2026) **[code]**: converts pretrained bidirectional video diffusion models into causal, action-conditioned world models via "video diffusion causalization" + "causal action guidance"; FVD 106.6 on CS:GO; evaluated on robot manipulation, 3D games, open-world navigation; code at knightnemo.github.io/vid2world. — [arXiv 2505.14357](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.14357); [ICLR 2026](https://iclr.cc/virtual/2026/poster/10007346)
- **Odyssey-1** (May 28 2025) **[API/web preview]**: streaming 720p / 30 fps, next frame predicted 40 ms ahead, 2-minute sessions, GPU cost **$1–2 per user-hour**; artifacts (edge smearing, lighting wobble); browser preview at experience.odyssey.world. — [radiancefields](https://radiancefields.com/odyssey-unveils-interactive-video). **Odyssey-2** (Oct 27 2025) / Odyssey-2 Pro & Max: start from image or text, action-conditioned, new frame every **50 ms (~20 fps)**, "minutes of imagined video", type text mid-stream; API available for developers; pricing not on the docs page. — [odyssey.ml/introducing-odyssey-2](https://odyssey.ml/introducing-odyssey-2); [API docs](https://documentation.api.odyssey.ml/odyssey-2-overview)

**Camera-trajectory control (offline)**
- MotionCtrl (Dec 2023): camera extrinsics fed into diffusion; finetuned on RealEstate10K w/ BLIP-2 captions. — [arXiv 2312.03641](https://www.arxiv-vanity.com/papers/2312.03641)
- CameraCtrl (Apr 2024; ICLR 2025): plug-and-play camera module on frozen video diffusion; dataset study (diverse camera distribution matters). — [arXiv 2404.02101](https://arxiv.org/html/2404.02101v2)
- VD3D (Jul 2024): first Plücker-embedding ControlNet for transformer video diffusion; SOTA on RealEstate10K. — [arXiv 2407.12781](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.12781v3). CamI2V (Oct 2024): Plücker + epipolar attention, +25.5% controllability on RealEstate10K. — [arXiv 2410.15957](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.15957v1). CamPilot (Jan 2026): ControlNet on CogVideoX-5B-I2V with Plücker. — [alphaXiv 2601.16214](https://www.alphaxiv.org/overview/2601.16214). CameraNoise (2026) argues numeric camera injection yields weak sensitivity; CamTrol is training-free with lower rotation error than MotionCtrl/CameraCtrl. — [alphaXiv 2605.30774](https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2605.30774.md)
- ReCamMaster (Kuaishou; Mar 2025): re-renders an **existing video** from a new camera path (video-to-video). — [arXiv 2503.11647](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.11647v1)
- **GEN3C** (NVIDIA; CVPR 2025 Highlight; code+weights Jun 6 2025) **[OPEN]**: 7B Cosmos-based; builds a **3D cache** (point clouds from predicted depth of seed images) and conditions on its renderings along a user camera path; any image via `--input_image_path`; 121 frames default (121×N−1 for longer); multiview example 704×1280; ~43 GB with offloading (H100/A100 tested); **code Apache-2.0, weights NVIDIA Open Model License**. — [GitHub nv-tlabs/GEN3C](https://github.com/nv-tlabs/GEN3C)
- **HunyuanWorld-Voyager** (Tencent; Sep 2 2025) **[OPEN, license has restrictions — unverified]**: single image + camera path (forward/back/left/right/turn_left/turn_right or custom) → **RGB-D video** + world-consistent point cloud sequences; 49 frames per clip, 540p (512×768 benchmark); ~60 GB peak VRAM, 80 GB GPU recommended; 50-step inference **288 s on 8×H20, 1,925 s on 1 GPU**; "world cache with point culling" for iterative extension; PLY point-cloud export; Japanese guide notes regional/commercial license restrictions. — [GitHub HunyuanWorld-Voyager](https://github.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/HunyuanWorld-Voyager); [weel.co.jp](https://weel.co.jp/media/tech/hunyuanworld-voyager/)

**Accept arbitrary user photo as start?**
- Yes (documented CLI image input): Matrix-Game 2.0 (`--img_path`), HY-WorldPlay (I2V image path required), Yume (`--jpg_dir`), GEN3C (`--input_image_path`), Voyager (single image), Hunyuan-GameCraft-1.0 (single image), Odyssey-2 (image or text), Genie 3 (text prompt world description; image start not stated on blog). — sources as above.

### Inferences
- Real-time interactive generation at ≥720p currently costs multi-GPU inference (Matrix-Game 3.0: 9 GPUs; Odyssey: $1–2/user-hour on their infra). Single-GPU open models top out around 480p–540p at 12–25 fps (HY-WorldPlay 480p/24 fps on 28–72 GB; Yume-1.5 540p/12 fps on one A100; Matrix-Game 2.0 ~640×352/25 fps on ≥24 GB).
- For a hobby/indie budget, Matrix-Game 2.0 (MIT, 24 GB) and Yume-5B (Apache-2.0, 16 GB) are the only options that fit a single consumer GPU; HY-WorldPlay 5B-WAN variant may also fit but with degraded control/memory.
- Game-trained models (GameCraft, Matrix-Game) are biased toward third/first-person game footage from Unreal/AAA; HY-WorldPlay and Yume include real-world video so should generalize better to a user's real photo, but this was not benchmarked in sources I read.

### Gaps
- Wan-VACE, Runway Gen-3/Gen-4 camera-control parameters, "X-Dyna", and Pika were not researched in depth (no primary-source hits in the budgeted calls).
- Matrix-Game 3.0 license (repo says MIT at top level; model weights license not confirmed), max sequence length, and whether a real photo works are not stated.
- GameCraft-2 resolution/fps/GPU and release status unknown; InterBench numbers not read from the paper.
- Odyssey-2 API pricing and session limits not found on official docs.

## KQ3. Scene persistence: keeping the same place consistent when you turn around and come back

### Takeaway
Four mechanisms are in use: (1) **retrieved-frame memory keyed by camera pose / FOV overlap** (WorldMem, Context-as-Memory, Matrix-Game 3.0, HY-WorldPlay "Reconstituted Context Memory"); (2) **geometry-indexed view memory** (VMem surfels); (3) **explicit 3D cache rendered back as conditioning** (GEN3C point clouds, Voyager world cache, EgoSim persistent 3D state); (4) **emergent memory in a large model** (Genie 3 ≈1 minute). Open models claim "minute-long" consistency; none of the sources quantify behavior beyond a few minutes, and Context-as-Memory explicitly notes error accumulation remains open.

### Cited Findings
- **WorldMem** (NTU/PKU/Shanghai AI Lab; arXiv Apr 2025; NeurIPS 2025): memory bank of "memory units" (frames + poses + timestamps); state-aware memory attention retrieves relevant frames to reconstruct previously seen scenes after large viewpoint/time gaps; timestamps allow modeling dynamic evolution; evaluated in virtual (Minecraft-style) and real scenarios. — [arXiv 2504.12369](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.12369); [NeurIPS 2025](https://neurips.cc/virtual/2025/poster/117127)
- **Context-as-Memory** (HKU/ZJU/Kuaishou Kling; Jun 2025): stores historical frames as-is and concatenates them along the frame dimension as input (no extra control modules); **Memory Retrieval** picks context frames by camera-pose **FOV overlap**; limited to static scenes; error accumulation over long videos remains open; a third-party paper notes code/weights **not released**. — [arXiv 2506.03141](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.03141v1); [alphaXiv MemCam overview](https://www.alphaxiv.org/overview/2603.26193)
- **VMem** (Oxford; ICCV 2025): indexes past views by the 3D surfels they observed and retrieves only views relevant to the current camera, avoiding both the error accumulation of outpaint+reconstruct pipelines and the short context of plain video models. — [arXiv 2506.18903](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.18903v3); [ICCV 2025](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2025/html/Li_VMem_Consistent_Interactive_Video_Scene_Generation_with_Surfel-Indexed_View_Memory_ICCV_2025_paper.html)
- **MemCam** (Mar 2026): memory-augmented camera control for consistent generation. — [alphaXiv 2603.26193](https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2603.26193)
- **Matrix-Game 3.0** (Mar 2026): camera-aware memory retrieval by pose + FOV overlap; retrieved frames encoded with relative Plücker encoding and jointly attended; first frame kept as a persistent "sink latent" anchor; consistency claimed over "minute-long sequences". — [project page](https://matrix-game-v3.github.io/)
- **HY-WorldPlay** (Dec 2025): "Reconstituted Context Memory" rebuilds context from past frames and uses temporal reframing to keep geometrically important long-past frames accessible; marketed as reducing memory decay. — [GitHub](https://github.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/HY-WorldPlay)
- **Genie 3** (Aug 2025): consistency called an *emergent* capability without explicit 3D representation; visual memory "as far back as one minute"; worlds stay largely consistent for "several minutes" (press reports vary between "about a minute" and "several minutes"). — [DeepMind blog](https://deepmind.google/discover/blog/genie-3-a-new-frontier-for-world-models/); [heise](https://heise.de/-10511028)
- **GEN3C** 3D cache (point clouds from depth of seed images, re-rendered along the path) and **Voyager** world cache with point culling give geometry-grounded persistence for camera-only exploration. — [GEN3C](https://github.com/nv-tlabs/GEN3C); [Voyager](https://github.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/HunyuanWorld-Voyager)
- **EgoSim** (Apr 2026) persistently updates an explicit 3D scene state for closed-loop egocentric interaction; **EgoGenesis** (Jul 2026) uses "online anchored projective memory". — [arXiv 2604.01001](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.01001); [arXiv 2607.28243](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.28243)
- **Lyra 2.0** (NVIDIA, reported Apr 2026): generates long camera-guided video autoregressively (80 frames/step) explicitly targeting "spatial forgetting" and "temporal drifting", then reconstructs a 3DGS + mesh so the final world is a persistent asset. — [i-scoop](https://www.i-scoop.eu/nvidia-lyra-2-0-generates-explorable-3d-worlds-from-a-single-image/)

### Inferences
- For a game where the player must return to the same spot after minutes (e.g., return to the hole you dug), **explicit 3D (splat/mesh/point cache) is the only approach with unbounded persistence**; retrieved-frame memory degrades with drift and is only validated at minute scale. A hybrid (3D world for static layout + video model for dynamic events) is the realistic design.
- Retrieval-by-FOV-overlap (Context-as-Memory, Matrix-Game 3.0) is cheap to implement on top of any AR video model if you track camera pose; the cost is that dynamic changes (dug hole, picked-up object) must be re-injected as updated memory frames or they revert.

### Gaps
- No source gives a quantitative "revisit consistency after N minutes" benchmark comparing these systems; WorldScore-style benchmarks exist for 3D generation but not for memory.
- WorldMem code/weights status not checked.

## KQ4. Text-triggered events mid-stream ("a wolf appears", "the ground cracks open")

### Takeaway
Only interactive world models expose true mid-stream text injection: Genie 3 ("promptable world events", preview only), Odyssey-2 (type while streaming, API), Hunyuan-GameCraft-2 (text instructions + keys, unreleased), and Yume-1.5 (per-2-second caption lines, open). For a **pre-generate-clips** strategy, every major commercial API supports image-to-video from the current frame with a text prompt, and several support **first+last frame** (Veo 3.1, Kling 2.5/3.0, Seedance 1.5 Pro, LTX-2, Wan FLF2V) and **extend** (Veo 3.1: +7 s per step; Kling: +4–5 s per call up to 3 min; Sora 2 Pro up to 25 s).

### Cited Findings
- **Genie 3 promptable world events**: text prompts change a generated world mid-interaction (weather, add objects/characters); the blog notes events "are not necessarily performed by the agent itself" and action space is limited. — [DeepMind blog](https://deepmind.google/discover/blog/genie-3-a-new-frontier-for-world-models/)
- **Odyssey-2 Pro**: "type more words during a stream and the video adapts to your new request in that moment"; new frame every 50 ms. — [Odyssey API docs](https://documentation.api.odyssey.ml/odyssey-2-overview)
- **Hunyuan-GameCraft-2**: instructions like "draw a torch", "hold a gun and fire" plus WASD/mouse; categories: environmental changes (weather, explosions), actor actions (draw weapon, open door), entity appearances (vehicles, animals); long-video gallery shows interaction prompts "that keep extending the same timeline". — [arXiv 2511.23429](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.23429); [project page](https://hunyuan-gamecraft-2.github.io/)
- **Yume / Yume-1.5**: text captions one per line each controlling a 2-second segment; Yume-1.5 adds text control over world events; prompt refinement with InternVL-2B. — [GitHub](https://github.com/stdstu12/YUME); [arXiv 2512.22096](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.22096v1)
- **Veo 3.1** (Gemini API, Oct 2025 launch): scene extension generates a new clip conditioned on the **final second** of the previous clip; third-party docs: each extension adds **7 s**, chainable up to 20× (~148 s), must match aspect ratio/resolution, only Veo-generated videos; **first+last frame** via `image` + `lastFrame`; **up to 3 reference images** ("ingredients") for character/object/scene consistency; reference images and first/last frame are mutually exclusive; extension does not accept reference images; a developer forum reports docs/API inconsistency on reference-image support. — [Google developers blog](https://developers.googleblog.com/en/introducing-veo-3-1-and-new-creative-controls-in-the-gemini-api); [scenario.com guide](https://help.scenario.com/articles/1049410120-veo-3-1-the-essentials); [eachlabs extend](https://www.eachlabs.ai/google/veo3-1/veo3-1-extend-video); [forum thread](https://discuss.ai.google.dev/t/veo-3-1-reference-images-docs-say-available-api-says-not-supported/111853)
- **Kling**: 2.5 Turbo Pro supports a "tail image" for start+end-frame animation and prompt camera direction (no audio); 3.0 Pro: 3–15 s, multi-prompt scene sequencing, "custom elements" for character consistency, native audio; start/end frame on the same endpoint as T2V *(third-party)*; **video-extend endpoint** takes a video ID and text prompt, adds ~4–5 s per call, total ≤3 min *(third-party)*. — [fal I2V guide](https://fal.ai/learn/tools/ai-image-to-video-generators); [ComfyUI KlingVideoExtendNode](https://docs.comfy.org/built-in-nodes/KlingVideoExtendNode.md); [atlascloud length guide](https://www.test.atlascloud.ai/blog/tips/kling-ai-video-length-limit)
- **Seedance 1.5 Pro** (fal): start+end-frame control; pan/tilt/zoom/dolly/orbit/tracking and fixed-camera options; 4–12 s; 1080p; native audio. — [fal I2V guide](https://fal.ai/learn/tools/ai-image-to-video-generators)
- **LTX-2-19B** (Lightricks, open weights, on fal): end-image URL for start+end frames; LoRA training; 121 frames. **PixVerse v5.6**: transition endpoints between two images. — same fal guide.
- **Sora 2 Pro** (fal): 4/8/12 s standard, up to 25 s extended; one Sept 2026 comparison states Sora 2 shuts down Sept 24 2026 — treat as unverified. — [fal guide](https://fal.ai/learn/tools/ai-image-to-video-generators); [teamday.ai](https://www.teamday.ai/blog/best-ai-video-models-2026)
- **Wan FLF2V** (Alibaba, Wan2.1 line): open-source first+last-frame model, Apache-2.0, 720p *(secondary host page)*; Wan2.2 FLF via "Fun Inpaint" high/low-noise variants on Hugging Face. — [atlascloud wan-flf2v](https://www.atlascloud.ai/id/models/alibaba/wan-flf2v); [RunComfy Wan2.2 FLF](https://www.runcomfy.com/playground/community/wan-2-2/fun-inp/first-last-frame)
- **Runway Aleph** (`gen4_aleph`): video-to-video editing/transformation of an existing clip, 15 credits/s. — [Runway API pricing docs](https://docs.dev.runwayml.com/guides/pricing)
- ReCamMaster (open) re-renders an existing clip under a new camera, a video-to-video editing route. — [arXiv 2503.11647](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.11647v1)

### Inferences
- Practical "dig → treasure appears" pipeline today: capture the current frame → I2V with prompt (or first+last frame where the last frame is an image-edited "treasure revealed" keyframe) → splice. Veo 3.1 (ingredients + FLF + extend) and Kling 3.0 (elements + extend) have the richest consistency toolset; Seedance 1.5 Pro and LTX-2 are the cheapest with FLF.
- Mid-stream injection in open models is limited to Yume (2-second caption granularity) and, if released, GameCraft-2; a true "promptable event" at interactive rate is otherwise only available via Odyssey-2 API.

### Gaps
- No official Kling API doc was reachable; extend/FLF details come from resellers and ComfyUI.
- Latency of extension calls (seconds of wall-clock per generated second) was not published by any provider I found.
- Veo 3.1 reference-image availability on Vertex vs Gemini API conflicting.

## KQ5. Single image → explorable 3D (splats/NeRF/mesh) and hybrids

### Takeaway
Single-image-to-3DGS has become fast: FlashWorld produces a splat scene in **~4–7 s on one H100/A100** (Apache-2.0, open), Lyra (NVIDIA, open code) decodes 3DGS from video-diffusion latents, and World Labs **Marble** is a hosted product exporting SPZ/PLY splats and GLB meshes on paid tiers. Larger "walkable" worlds come from autoregressive video + reconstruction (Lyra 2.0, Voyager, GEN3C), at minutes per scene. All splats render in real time in three.js (native r186 renderer; Spark MIT/WebGL2), Unity (aras-p plugin) and Unreal (XScene plugin). Nobody delivers *interactive* dynamics in the 3D route, hence hybrids.

### Cited Findings
- **World Labs Marble** **[API/product]**: exports Gaussian splats as SPZ and PLY; Free plan generates but cannot export; Standard plan: splat export, 360 panoramas, collider meshes; Pro: high-quality textured GLB mesh + commercial rights; integrations listed: Spark (three.js), Unreal, Unity, Blender, Houdini. — [docs.worldlabs.ai export](https://docs.worldlabs.ai/marble/export/gaussian-splat/index.md). Third-party: Standard $20/mo (12 generations), Pro $35, Max $95 *(secondary)*; API credits $1.00 per 1,250, HQ mesh export 3,500 credits, splats ~2M or 500K per export *(secondary, flagged unverified by its own source)*; my fetch of marble.worldlabs.ai/pricing returned only a title (JS page). — [aixploria](https://www.aixploria.com/?p=33901); [wireflow](https://www.wireflow.ai/docs/nodes/3d--marble)
- **Spark** (World Labs, MIT): three.js 3DGS renderer on WebGL2 (~98% of devices incl. phones/VR); Spark 2.0 adds LoD streaming for huge worlds; formats .PLY/.SPZ/.SPLAT/.KSPLAT/.SOG. — [sparkjs.dev](https://sparkjs.dev/docs/overview/); [Spark 2.0 blog](https://www.worldlabs.ai/blog/spark-2.0)
- **three.js r186** merged a native WebGPU/TSL Gaussian splat renderer; **GaussianSplats3D** community renderer reads PLY/.splat/.ksplat. — [radiancefields](https://radiancefields.com/three.js-merges-a-native-gaussian-splat-renderer-for-webgpu-in-r186); [npm gaussian-splats-3d](https://npmjs.org/package/gaussian-splats-3d)
- **Unity**: aras-p/UnityGaussianSplatting renders PLY and SPZ across Built-in/URP/HDRP; Simulacrum Splat Box Editor $199 with relighting/shadows. **Unreal**: XScene-UEPlugin (Niagara-based, hybrid rendering). **Editor**: PlayCanvas SuperSplat (MIT) for floater cleanup and export. — [UnityGaussianSplatting docs](https://docsearch.algolia.com/mcp/docs/repo/aras-p/unitygaussiansplatting); [SplatBus paper citing XScene](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.15431v1); [PlayCanvas docs](https://developer.playcanvas.com/user-manual/graphics/gaussian-splatting)
- **HunyuanWorld-1.0** (Tencent; Jul 26 2025; Lite Aug 15 2025) **[OPEN, license w/ restrictions]**: text or image → 360° panorama "world proxy" → semantically layered 3D **mesh** (Draco-compressed export; browser viewer); Lite quantized version runs on RTX 4090; WonderZoom's table measures 704 s per scene. — [GitHub HunyuanWorld-1.0](https://github.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/HunyuanWorld-1.0); [WonderZoom](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2512.09164)
- **FlashWorld** (Oct 16 2025; ICLR 2026 oral) **[OPEN, Apache-2.0]**: text or single image → 3DGS in **~7 s (A100/A800) / ~4 s (H100)**; default ~51 GB VRAM, offload modes 30/24/9 GB (slower, up to 10 min); exports SPZ/PLY + optional video; HF Space demo; training code by form. — [GitHub imlixinyang/FlashWorld](https://github.com/imlixinyang/FlashWorld); [arXiv 2510.13678](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.13678v1)
- **Lyra** (NVIDIA; code Sep 23 2025; ICLR 2026) **[OPEN, NVIDIA Open Model License]**: 3DGS decoder trained in a video diffusion model's latent space via self-distillation (no multi-view GT); single image or text → 3DGS for real-time rendering; monocular video → dynamic 4D; H100/A100, ~43 GB *(secondary)*. — [NVIDIA page](https://research.nvidia.com/labs/toronto-ai/lyra); [arXiv 2509.19296](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.19296); [HF nvidia/Lyra](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/Lyra/blob/refs%2Fpr%2F2/README.md)
- **Lyra 2.0** (reported Apr 17 2026): single image → long AR camera-guided video (80 frames/step) → feed-forward 3DGS (Depth Anything v3-based, finetuned on generated scenes) + mesh via OpenVDB/marching cubes; ~**194 s/step on one GB200, ~15 s/step distilled**; trained on 64 GB200s; static scenes only; ~90 m coverage claim unverified; license conflicting (Product Hunt says Apache-2.0 *(secondary)*). — [i-scoop](https://www.i-scoop.eu/nvidia-lyra-2-0-generates-explorable-3d-worlds-from-a-single-image/); [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/products/lyra-2-0-by-nvidia)
- **WonderWorld** (CVPR 2025) **[OPEN]**: layered Gaussian surfels with geometry-based init, <1 s per layer; 9.3 s per new scene in WonderZoom's table; **WonderZoom** (Dec 2025) 62.1 s per scale and 83.8% human preference over Gen3C; Gen3C 306.7 s and Voyager 596.6 s per scene in that table. — [WonderWorld CVPR](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025/papers/Yu_WonderWorld_Interactive_3D_Scene_Generation_from_a_Single_Image_CVPR_2025_paper.pdf); [WonderZoom](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2512.09164)
- **Gen3D** (Nov 2025): WorldScore 75.05 vs WonderWorld 72.69, LucidDreamer 70.40, WonderJourney 63.75. — [arXiv 2511.14291](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2511.14291)
- FlashWorld paper groups **CAT3D, Bolt3D, Wonderland** as multi-view-diffusion methods that "suffer from noisy textures due to multi-view inconsistency" (qualitative). — [FlashWorld](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.13678v1)
- **Matrix-3D** mentioned only as a panorama-video → navigable-world pipeline (optimization path or large panorama reconstruction model). — [WonderZoom](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2512.09164)
- Hybrids: Voyager (RGB-D video + world cache → PLY point cloud), GEN3C (3D cache + video), Lyra 2.0 (video → 3DGS + mesh), EgoSim (video + persistent 3D state), HunyuanWorld 1.0 → HY-World 1.5/WorldPlay (static mesh world → real-time interactive video model, same team). — sources above; [HY-WorldPlay](https://github.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/HY-WorldPlay)

### Inferences
- A viable architecture: one-shot FlashWorld/Marble splat of the user's photo (seconds) rendered in three.js/Unity for free movement with perfect persistence; video model (Kling/Veo I2V from the rendered view, or an open AR model) only for event clips; optionally bake event results back by re-splatting.
- Splat quality falls off quickly beyond the input viewpoint; Lyra 2.0 / Voyager / WonderZoom extend coverage but at minutes per scene on datacenter GPUs. Exported splats (SPZ/PLY) are engine-agnostic today.
- Licensing: FlashWorld (Apache-2.0) and WonderWorld are the cleanest for commercial use; Lyra/GEN3C weights are under the NVIDIA Open Model License; Hunyuan models carry regional/commercial clauses (not read).

### Gaps
- Bolt3D, CAT3D/CAT4D, ZeroNVS, ViewCrafter, DimensionX, SpatialGen specifics (time, license, availability) not retrieved.
- Marble generation time and official plan prices not readable from the pricing page (JS-rendered).
- Lyra 2.0 official repo/license not located.

## KQ6. Commercial video APIs for pre-generated result clips: features, latency, $/s

Nearly all support image-to-video; first+last frame is available on Veo 3.1, Kling 2.5/3.0, Seedance 1.5 Pro, LTX-2; extension on Veo 3.1, Kling, Sora 2; reference-image consistency on Veo 3.1 (3 images) and Kling 3.0 (elements). **No provider publishes generation latency**; I found no reliable wall-clock figures.

- Because no latency SLAs are published and Odyssey characterizes typical generation as "minutes", the pre-generation strategy must budget tens of seconds to minutes per clip and should branch-predict likely actions.
